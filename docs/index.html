What the architectures mean

AM (endo) fungi pierce into root cells and form arbuscules — the textbook phosphate-for-carbon bargain across most land plants. EM (ecto) fungi wrap roots in a mantle and Hartig net; they are famous on pines and many forest trees, and often excel at mining organic nitrogen from litter. Neither path is Hyphaneural gene-follow or Cambium sap physiology — this desk stays on the underground trade.
