Mycorrhizal Mesh

Watch trees and mycorrhizal fungi trade carbon for phosphorus and nitrogen across a living mesh.

Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi grow into root cells and trade soil phosphorus and nitrogen for sugars the tree fixed from sunlight.

Trade notes

In Advanced view, particle density rises and the season control slows the exchange when the canopy is dormant. Carbon still moves toward the fungus; phosphorus and nitrogen still move toward the tree. Quantities here are qualitative teaching aids, not measured field fluxes.
